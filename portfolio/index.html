Selected Work

Three shows, three visual languages.

Each campaign needed to stand on its own while still reading as part of the same station. Below is the brief, the approach, and the tools behind each poster.

88.1 FM

Flagship Show Campaign

Global Ghana

The flagship show needed a poster that read instantly as "Ghanaian music, worldwide reach." I built the layout around a two-tone map in the station's red and green, anchored by a tight lineup of the real hosts so the show felt personal, not stock. A bold cream display face keeps the headline legible over the busy background at social-feed size.

RoleSole Designer
ToolsPhotoshop, Illustrator
FormatSocial + In-Studio
92.7 FM

Genre Segment Campaign

Ghana Drill Vibes

Drill needed to feel rawer than the flagship brand without breaking from it. I pushed a red-to-green duotone over documentary-style photography of the artists, then paired it with hand-lettered, marker-style type to match the genre's street energy — same palette family, completely different register.

RoleSole Designer
ToolsPhotoshop, Hand Lettering
FormatSocial + In-Studio
100.5 FM

Artist Feature Poster

Fameye Live

A single-artist feature for an exclusive on-air interview and performance. The diagonal color-blocking pulls directly from the Ghanaian flag palette, while a centered typographic lockup and clean portrait cutout keep the focus on the artist and tie the poster back into the wider station identity.

RoleSole Designer
ToolsPhotoshop, Illustrator
FormatSocial + In-Studio
